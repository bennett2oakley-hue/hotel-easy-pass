Privacy

Hotel Easy Pass is designed to keep trip information on your device. Hotel details, rewards, notes, checklists, packing items and local usage events are stored in your browser's local storage unless you choose to share or open information.

Links

The app may open third-party travel services such as booking, flights and experiences. Those services have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or tracking when you leave Hotel Easy Pass.

Usage events

The app currently stores a small local activity log on your device for features such as saving a stay, searching and marketplace clicks. It is not a cross-user analytics system and is not sent to our server.

Changes

This page may be updated as the app adds analytics, advertising or affiliate partnerships. Any future third-party tracking should be disclosed here.
