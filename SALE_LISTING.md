# Hotel Easy Pass Traveler — Sale Listing

A standalone, mobile-first trip companion for hotel guests and travelers. Helps users organize stays, keep travel details together, track rewards, build checklists, manage trip budgets and tips, document room condition with photos, and reach emergency travel shortcuts.

**Included:** Traveler PWA, trip dashboard, travel utilities, browser-local notes, room photo documentation, installable app shell, privacy/terms pages, Supabase Auth/entitlement integration source, Stripe Checkout/webhook source, tests and deployment server.

**Not included:** hotel inventory or booking engine, guaranteed live rates/availability, PMS or smart-lock integration, or the separate Hotel Owner product.

**Launch status:** Source and smoke tests are maintained in the `traveler-app` branch. Real payments are not active until the separate Traveler Stripe account, test prices, webhook, and dedicated Supabase credentials are configured and verified. Do not describe billing as live before end-to-end test completion.
