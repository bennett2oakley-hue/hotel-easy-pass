# Hotel Easy Pass Monetization Roadmap

## Revenue engine
Hotel Easy Pass is structured to monetize travel intent rather than charge users for basic utility features.

### Primary revenue channels
1. Hotel booking affiliate/revenue-share links.
2. Experiences and attraction affiliate links.
3. Flight and transportation referrals.
4. Local partner offers and sponsored placements.
5. Optional premium features later, once usage is established.

## Partner integration
The app currently includes a visible Travel Marketplace section with partner-ready outbound links. Replace the public partner URLs with approved tracking/deep links after affiliate accounts are approved. Keep a clear affiliate disclosure in the app.

Travelpayouts supports travel affiliate tools including links, widgets, search forms, white-label products and APIs. Wink also advertises partner hotel booking infrastructure with attribution and commission options. Verify current terms, approval requirements and rates before using either service.

## Growth loop
- Make the free utility layer genuinely useful.
- Give users a one-tap Share action.
- Create destination-specific landing pages/content over time.
- Track visits, marketplace clicks and completed bookings.
- Use real usage and revenue metrics as the core evidence of business value for a future sale.

## Buyer-ready metrics to build
- Monthly active users
- Returning users
- Marketplace click-through rate
- Confirmed bookings
- Monthly revenue
- Revenue per active user
- Acquisition source
- Email/social audience size
- Operating costs and profit

Do not represent projected revenue as actual revenue. The value of the business will depend heavily on verified traffic, retention, revenue, ownership of the code/assets, and transferable partner relationships.
