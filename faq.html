HOTEL EASY PASS

Travel questions, answered.

A free, phone-friendly toolkit for hotel stays, nearby places, budgets, rewards, packing and trip notes.

What is Hotel Easy Pass?

It puts common hotel-trip tasks in one place so you can save stay details, find nearby services, track a trip budget, manage checklists and share your trip information.

Does it cost anything?

The core Hotel Easy Pass toolkit is free to use. Some travel links may send you to third-party services where prices and terms are set by those providers.

Does it store my hotel password or credit card?

No. The app is designed to store practical trip information locally in your browser. Do not enter passwords, payment-card numbers or other highly sensitive information into trip notes.

Can I use it on Android?

Yes. It is designed for mobile browsers and can be installed as a web app when your browser offers installation. If an install prompt appears, use it. Otherwise use your browser's “Add to Home screen” option.

Can I share my hotel details?

Yes. Save your stay and use Share Trip. Your phone's sharing options can send the saved hotel name, room, address and check-in/check-out details. Review the information before sending it.

How does Hotel Easy Pass make money?

The app has a marketplace area designed for travel partner referrals. At present, ordinary outbound links should not be treated as confirmed affiliate revenue. Approved partner tracking can be connected later.

Are prices and reservations guaranteed?

No. Third-party providers control their own inventory, prices, availability and booking terms. Confirm the details directly with the provider before paying or traveling.
