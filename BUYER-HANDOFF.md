## Current architecture
Static/mobile-first PWA. Core traveler and owner data is stored locally in the browser. There is no required database or server-side account system in the current version.

## What is NOT included unless separately agreed
- A registered domain name
- A production hotel booking API
- Affiliate accounts
- Payment processor accounts
- Hotel contracts
- Customer/user database
- Verified revenue
- Paid advertising accounts
- Third-party trademarks or partner rights

## Buyer due diligence checklist
1. Open the live demo.
2. Test the Traveler Pass on an Android phone.
3. Test the Hotel Owner Hub.
4. Inspect the repository.
5. Confirm GitHub Pages deployment.
6. Review the privacy and terms pages.
7. Confirm exactly which accounts, domains and external services are part of the purchase.
8. Use escrow or a marketplace payment/transfer process where available.
9. Only transfer repository ownership after the agreed payment/escrow conditions are satisfied.
